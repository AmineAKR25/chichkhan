# Havana — menu blend

Havana is the primary identity. Café Lounge is a browsing reference only.

Scope confirmed: category navigation, cross-catalogue search and responsive menu layouts. Follow-up adds a new, distinctive homepage. Existing 38 items, prices, six category routes, product pages and practical information remain the content foundation.

Palette: Nuit #14181a, deep charcoal #0e1113, cream #f4ede1, sand #e3d6c3, antique gold #c39b4a, terracotta #ce6d49. Existing chapter accents remain intact. Cormorant Garamond is the display voice; Jost is the functional typeface.

Homepage signature: an oversized Havana wordmark over a two-part composition: quiet editorial copy and coloured placeholders framed by the existing Havana arch. No actual or generated images are used. Six category links form the lower menu section. This keeps the arch specific to Havana while introducing a new composition.

Menu: persistent header, desktop category sidebar, mobile scrolling category rail, accessible native search dialog. Search links directly to product routes. Category content stays server-rendered and linkable. Product and information page designs are outside this iteration.

Quality: no document horizontal overflow at 375/768/1280px; keyboard access and modal focus; reduced motion; all original category and product URLs preserved. No new dependencies.
